Lead Management Flow

How an enquiry becomes a sale — from first capture to a closed order — and who owns it at every step.

Phase 1 — Desk
Channels Web · Instagram · Facebook · WhatsApp · Referral

At a glance

Capture Nothing slips Enquiries from the website and social channels land automatically; phone, WhatsApp and walk-in enquiries are entered on the desk.
Ownership Assigned on arrival Every lead gets an owner the moment it’s created — the previous salesperson for a returning customer, or the next in the brand’s rotation.
Pipeline A guided path Each lead moves through clear stages with a win-likelihood weighting, so the team always knows where a deal stands.
Handover Deliberate allocation When a visit is on the table, a manager assigns the lead to a store and a salesperson by hand — a considered decision, not a scramble.
01

The lead lifecycle

The spine below is the path a healthy lead follows to a sale. The percentage on each stage is its win-likelihood weighting. Dashed branches show where a lead can exit early.

Open
Lead created Automatic status

A new lead is captured and immediately given an owner. Its status is set to Open, ready to be worked.

10%
In Progress

The owner reaches the customer. Once contacted, the lead moves into active work.

If not contacted → Follow up

Up to 5 attempts are recorded with timestamps in the lead’s journey (calls & WhatsApp). If the customer still can’t be reached, the lead is marked Unqualified — Not Contacted.

20%
Qualified

There’s genuine interest and a real fit. The lead is worth pursuing.

Otherwise → Unqualified → Closed

If the lead doesn’t qualify, a reason is chosen and the lead is closed out.

25%
Prospecting

Active nurturing — sharing products, prices and options to move the customer toward a visit.

50%
Visit Planned

A store visit is on the calendar — the strongest signal of intent so far.

No visit → Unqualified

If no visit materialises, the lead is marked unqualified with a reason.

80%
Lead Allocation Manual · Desk

A manager hands the lead to a store and a salesperson. They pick the store, then choose from the salespeople at that store. This is a deliberate, manual decision.

100%
Closed Won Invoice auto-tagged

The sale is made. The related invoice is linked to the lead automatically. In store, the team completes the customer tagging & consent step that follows a purchase.

Or → Closed Lost

If the deal doesn’t come through, the lead closes as lost with a reason. Every lead ends as either Closed Won or Closed Lost.

02

How leads come in

Two routes, one destination — both create a lead ready to be owned and worked.

Automatic capture

Enquiries from the website, Instagram, Facebook and campaigns arrive on their own through the website enquiry connection. The customer’s details and the product they asked about are filled in automatically.

Captured automatically: name, email, phone, city, product, model, price, page, source & brand. Campaign reference stored as a tag.

Manual entry

For WhatsApp, incoming calls and referrals, the team creates the lead on the desk and notes where it came from.

Required: source, customer name, mobile, email. Optional: brand, product.
03

Who gets the lead

Every lead is assigned an owner the moment it’s created — the system first checks whether we already know this customer (by matching their mobile or email against past leads).

Returning customer

If we’ve dealt with them before for this brand, the lead goes straight back to the salesperson who last handled them — keeping the relationship intact. Brand rotation is skipped.

New customer

A brand-new customer — or a familiar one asking about a brand they haven’t enquired on before — is assigned by the brand’s rotation, so enquiries are shared evenly across the team.

Brand rotation, in turn

Each brand keeps its own list of salespeople. New leads are handed out one after another — first lead to the first person, next to the second, and so on. Once everyone has had one, it starts again from the top. Anyone marked unavailable is skipped.

ASalesperson 1 → BSalesperson 2 → CSalesperson 3 · next → DSalesperson 4
↻ Skips Salesperson 2 (marked unavailable) and loops back to the top after the last.
04

Working & handing over

Following up

Every call and WhatsApp touch is logged with a timestamp in the lead’s journey. A lead that can’t be reached after five attempts is closed out as unqualified — no lead lingers untouched.

Allocation by hand

At the visit stage a manager allocates the lead on the desk: choose the store, then pick a salesperson from that store. A single, accountable decision — there is no bidding or automatic grab.

Closing the sale

A won lead has its invoice linked automatically. The in-store team then completes customer tagging and consent — the routine that follows any purchase.

Every lead lands

No lead is left in limbo. Each one finishes in a definite state — Closed Won or Closed Lost — giving clean, reliable reporting.
